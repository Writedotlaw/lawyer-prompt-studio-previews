# Make the writing clearer without changing what it says.
> An effective revision helps the reader understand the point and its limits. Use AI to explore writing choices you can explain, while keeping control of the facts, reasoning, and emphasis.
@nav Improve legal writing

## Diagnose the reader's problem before requesting a rewrite.

“Make this clearer” is a reasonable place to begin, but it leaves the most important writing decisions unstated. Clearer in what way? Does the opening delay the answer? Does a sentence hide who acted? Does the paragraph state a rule without showing how the facts satisfy it?

Read the passage with its intended reader in mind. A client deciding whether to approve a dividend needs to know whether approval can be confirmed and what stands in the way. A paragraph that begins by recounting everything the lawyer reviewed may delay that answer even if every sentence is accurate.

Once you can name the problem, you can give more useful direction. Ask the tool to put the unresolved condition near the recommendation, show how the numbers affect the answer, or make the next step explicit. Then compare the result with the original. A revision can improve readability while overstating a conclusion, and that tradeoff deserves your attention.

## Choose a verb that explains what the sentence means.

The fee-table sentence below describes what the table “is intended to assist investors in understanding.” Before looking at the alternatives, ask what the table actually does for its reader. A verb such as “shows” may express that relationship more directly.

But watch what happens to the rest of the sentence. The original includes costs borne directly or indirectly. A shorter version should not lose that distinction or imply that the table includes only amounts investors pay directly. A strong verb improves the sentence only if the resulting sentence still communicates the intended information.

Write your version, then compare the alternatives and their explanations. There is more than one defensible revision. The useful exercise is deciding what each version helps the reader see.

## Use an example to teach a particular writing choice.

An abstract opening can make a concrete dispute difficult to picture. Compare an introduction about the “design, marketing, and sale” of a dog toy with one that tells the reader what the company made and how the toy resembles a whiskey bottle. The second approach gives the reader something to understand before asking them to confront the legal question.

When you provide an example to AI, explain that choice. Ask it to identify the actors and describe the relevant conduct, rather than imitate the memorable phrase that made the original enjoyable. The technique can carry into a different matter even when the tone cannot.

The same decision arises in everyday client writing. “We reviewed Section 6.04 and the leverage calculation” tells the reader what you did. “We cannot yet confirm that the dividend is permitted” tells the reader where the matter stands. Whether you lead with one or the other depends on the purpose, but you should make that choice deliberately.

## Put the unresolved condition where the reader needs it.

Stonebridge wants confirmation that it may pay a $12 million dividend. Its reported leverage ratio is below the contractual limit only because the calculation includes projected cost savings. The passage supplies the conditions those savings must meet, but says the supporting material has not been provided.

That is the problem your revision needs to explain. The reader should understand why the current record does not support confirmation and what information is needed next. A vague request for “further diligence” leaves the practical task unclear. A definitive statement that the dividend is prohibited goes beyond the supplied facts.

Keep the numbers and qualifications intact as you reorganize the advice. In particular, “no separate default has been identified” does not establish that no default exists. The exercise asks you to communicate the limits of the present analysis, not resolve them through a more decisive tone.

## Separate concerns that require different responses.

Falcon Medical wants to send a reporting package to Redwood, a prospective assignee. Redwood has signed an NDA, but its residuals clause may permit use that the credit agreement does not allow. The package also contains customer-level information beyond what Redwood currently needs.

Those concerns are related, but they are not interchangeable. Reducing the information in the package does not by itself establish that the NDA satisfies the agreement. Revising the NDA does not explain why unnecessary sensitive detail should be included. Your recommendation should let the reader see each question and the response it requires.

As you edit, make the relationship explicit. What needs to be confirmed before sending? What can be narrowed now? Preserve the uncertainty about the NDA instead of treating the fact of signature as the end of the inquiry.

## Ask for an explanation you can check against the revision.

A useful editing response should help you learn from the changes. Ask the tool what it moved, what it cut, and which edits might affect meaning. Then compare that account with the actual text. The explanation is another work product to assess; it may miss an important change.

```prompt
Before rewriting, explain what the reader needs to understand and what currently makes that difficult. Preserve the supplied facts, numbers, conditions, and uncertainty. Offer two versions with meaningfully different organization and explain what each emphasizes. Flag any proposed change that could affect the analysis for my decision. Do not add facts or legal conclusions.

Audience and purpose: [describe them].
Passage: [paste it].
```

After you select an approach, read the passage again without the surrounding conversation. Can the intended reader identify the recommendation, understand the reason, and tell what happens next? That is the document's job. The prompt should help you get there, but the final text must work on its own.
